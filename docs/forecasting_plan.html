The forecasting pivot

The platform stops being a trading system that reports forecasts and becomes a forecasting system that can optionally simulate a book. What changes, what stays exactly as it is, what gets frozen, and the features worth adding once the primary artifact is a measurement rather than an equity curve.

PremiseOne decision reorganizes the whole platform

No trades will happen. Tracking error is acceptable. The deliverable is forecast skill on historical data, established mathematically.

That single constraint changes what "correct" means. An execution model that is wrong by 30 basis points no longer matters. A cross-validation scheme that leaks four days of overlapping label still matters completely — more than before, because the forecast metric is the product, and a corrupted metric is not a degraded result but a meaningless one.

So the work splits cleanly. Everything about getting filled can be frozen. Everything about measuring skill honestly becomes central and needs to be better than it currently is.

The cheapest large win, found while measuring

Every cached file starts on 2021-08-09 and ends 2026-08-07 — precisely five years, because default_history_years: 5 flows into start_date = end_date − years×365. That window is a configuration choice, not a limit of the source.

The consequence is severe for a forecasting platform: the sample begins after the COVID crash. It holds one bull run, one rate-hike correction, and no crisis. Every tail estimate, regime model and drawdown forecast is fitted on data containing no crash.

Widening the window costs one config value and a re-download.

Why this resolves A3 without decomposing anything

The architecture register flagged BacktestEngine — 2,412 lines, 47 methods, nine responsibilities — as critical, and proposed extracting collaborators over several PRs. Under the forecasting premise there is a cheaper answer: route around it. Order creation, fills, tax lots, circuit breakers, Kelly sizing and sector caps are machinery for a book that will never trade. Building the forecast harness beside the engine rather than inside it delivers the same benefit — testable evaluation, no god class in the critical path — for a fraction of the work, and leaves the engine intact for the day a portfolio question is actually asked.

Freeze, do not delete

Deleting is tempting and wrong. The value in the order path is the accumulated correctness — gap-aware stop fills, holiday handling, the delisting logic — none of which is cheap to rebuild. Move it to an execution/ namespace with a README stating it is not maintained under the current premise, exclude it from CI's required checks, and leave it working.

Part 4 · AdditionsFeatures worth adding

Ordered by return on effort for a mathematics-and-financial-analysis platform. The first four are the ones that make the difference between a forecast number and a defensible one.

Purged & embargoed cross-validation 3 days · essential

Predicting a 5-day return from daily observations means consecutive samples share four days of outcome. Standard CV trains on data overlapping its own test fold and reports skill that does not exist. Purging removes the overlapping samples; embargoing adds a gap so serial correlation cannot bridge the boundary.

Everything else on this page is measured through this. Without it, every other number is optimistic by an amount nobody can bound.

Forecast evaluation harness 1 week · essential

Rank IC and ICIR by horizon; decile spread and monotonicity; hit rate; forecast error distributions. Monotonicity across deciles is the check that catches a signal driven entirely by its extreme tail — a common and expensive illusion.

This is the artifact that replaces the equity curve, and it is what lets the backtest engine leave the critical path.

Signal decay curves 2 days · high value

IC as a function of forecast horizon — 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21 days. Answers how long the edge persists, which determines rebalancing frequency and therefore whether an edge survives costs at all. It also distinguishes a genuine slow signal from a fast one that is mostly microstructure noise.

Neutralized IC — sector, beta, size 2 days · high value

Compute IC after regressing out sector, market beta and size. The gap between raw and neutralized IC is the share of the signal that is a factor tilt rather than stock selection. Indian momentum concentrates hard by sector, so this is where a supposed alpha most often turns out to be an IT or PSU-bank bet.

Fama–MacBeth cross-sectional regressions 3 days · high value

The classical financial-econometrics answer to "does this characteristic price". Cross-sectional regression each date, then time-series inference on the coefficient series. You already have Newey–West standard errors in risk_analytics.py, which is the harder half — the autocorrelation correction is exactly what overlapping windows require.

This gives a t-statistic on a factor premium rather than a backtest, which is a far stronger form of evidence and much harder to overfit.

Gradient-boosted trees as the baseline model 2 days · high value

On tabular panel data at this sample size, GBMs typically beat sequence networks, train in seconds rather than minutes, and give honest feature importances. The LSTM is the more interesting model and the less appropriate default; make boosting the thing every new idea must beat.

Registers as a trainer alongside supervised and sac with no framework changes.

Seed ensembling 1 day · cheap

Average five seeds of one model. Reliably buys more out-of-sample stability than finding a better architecture, and costs only compute. It also gives a free estimate of how much of a result is initialization luck — which on this sample size is more than people expect.

Regime-conditional evaluation 3 days · high value

IC split by volatility regime, market direction and dispersion. A signal that works only in one regime is a different product from one that works throughout, and the aggregate number hides which you have. This is where the dormant Markov regime module earns its place.

Conformal prediction intervals 4 days · differentiating

Isotonic calibration gives a calibrated point probability. Conformal gives an interval with finite-sample coverage guarantees that hold without distributional assumptions — which matters because financial returns violate every distributional assumption normally made. For a forecasting platform the interval is the more honest output.

Combinatorial purged CV 1 week · advanced

Multiple train/test path combinations rather than one, giving a distribution of out-of-sample outcomes instead of a single number. Feeds PBO directly, and PBO is already built. The right upgrade once purged CV is in place and the basics are trusted.

Auto-generated research notes 3 days · quality of life

Each run manifest renders to a short HTML page: config, universe fingerprint, metrics, decay curve, IC by regime. Makes results comparable across weeks and stops the accumulation of undocumented experiments that no one can reproduce.

Part 5 · SequenceOrder of work

Week 1

Data, cheap and high-leverage

Widen the history window and refetch. Stop discarding adj_close, dividends, stock_splits. Add ingest invariants.

Checkpoint: report the new span, and how many corporate actions per year the recovered columns reveal. If the source only holds five years, that finding changes the plan and is worth knowing on day two rather than month two.

Week 2

Purged CV and the forecast harness

These land together because neither is meaningful alone. First measurement to produce: rank IC of the existing LSTM under purged CV, against the same model under the current walk-forward.

Checkpoint: the gap between those two numbers is the leakage you have been reporting as skill. Expect it to be uncomfortable.

Week 3

Decay, neutralization, boosting baseline

Signal decay curves, sector/beta/size-neutralized IC, and gradient boosting as the model every idea must beat.

Checkpoint: raw versus neutralized IC for each existing strategy. This tells you which of the six are stock selection and which are factor tilts.

Week 4

Platform hygiene

A1, A2, CLI smoke tests, run manifests. Freeze the execution namespace with its README. Small, mechanical, and it makes everything after this portable and reproducible.

Depth

  • Regime-conditional evaluation, wiring the Markov module in as a conditioning variable

Part 6 · HonestyWhat this plan does not fix

Stated plainly, because a plan that implies otherwise is worse than no plan.

  • Survivorship remains until point-in-time membership exists. Widening history helps the sample size and adds crises; it does not remove the bias. Under a forecasting premise you can at least measure around it — report IC within the available universe and state the bias direction — but a cross-sectional return claim stays unsafe.
  • Delisted price history is a separate acquisition from membership, and easy to conflate with it. Membership tells you a name was in the index; you still need its prices to evaluate a forecast that included it.
  • Indian data before roughly 2010 is thin and of uneven quality. Widening the window will not give you thirty clean years, and the earliest part of whatever you get deserves scepticism.
  • One market, one country. Every conclusion is a single realization of Indian equities. Cross-market validation is the only real answer and it is out of scope.

What success looks like

Concretely, so it is checkable rather than aspirational. By the end of week 3 you should be able to state, for every one of the six strategies:

  • Rank IC and ICIR under purged CV, with a Newey–West t-statistic
  • How much of it survives sector and beta neutralization
  • How fast it decays with horizon
  • Whether it holds in more than one volatility regime
  • How it compares to a gradient-boosting baseline on identical features and splits

None of those five numbers requires a single simulated trade — and together they are a far stronger statement about forecasting skill than any equity curve this platform has produced.
